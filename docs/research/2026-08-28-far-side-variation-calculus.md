# 3. Nucleus B — interaction is failure of parallel openings to agree

Consider toggling `A` under different settings of `B`, holding `C=c` fixed:

```math
\Delta_A F(0,c)
```

versus:

```math
\Delta_A F(1,c).
```

Their difference is:

```math
\Delta_B\Delta_A F(c)
=F(1,1,c)-F(1,0,c)-F(0,1,c)+F(0,0,c).
```

This is the alternating four-cell calculation already represented by Dogram `rectangle@1` in numeric mode.

## EXACT

For a declared numeric response:

```text

```

means only:

> at this declared `C=c` slice, the measured A-effect is unchanged by the B setting.

A nonzero value means:

> at this declared slice, the measured A-effect depends on the B setting.

## COMPRESSION

> **Geometry says the openings are parallel. Interaction asks whether the response agrees.**

## REFUSAL

A nonzero mixed difference does not identify the physical, historical, semantic, or causal mechanism responsible for the interaction.
