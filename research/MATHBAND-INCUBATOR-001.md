# MATHBAND-INCUBATOR-001

Status: experimental research kernel. No public operator.

## Question

Can Dogram pressure-test a declared bridge between two mathematical formulations while preserving partiality, unmatched structure, residual disagreement, and decisive failure without promoting the bridge into global equivalence?

For the frozen v0 specimens: yes, within the declared finite probe families. The result is a deterministic comparison receipt, not a theorem of semantic identity and not a bridge-discovery claim.

## Constitutional contract

MathBand consumes already-constituted finite probe observations from two intact mathematical voices and classifies only the declared comparison surface.

```text
BRIDGE != IDENTITY.
PRESERVED UNDER P != GLOBALLY EQUIVALENT.
UNMAPPED != FALSE.
EXTRA != ERROR.
APPROXIMATE != EXACT.
DECISIVE FAILURE CANNOT BE OUTVOTED BY SURFACE SIMILARITY.
EVALUATOR != BAT ORACLE.
```

Probe order, assumptions, representation transforms, and decisive/load-bearing probes are declared before evaluation. The evaluator does not choose a discriminator after seeing the outputs and does not compute the discipline-specific mathematics used by the hostile test oracle.

## Calibration bridge

Voice A represents a complex number as

```math
z = a + bi
```

and applies multiplication by `i`:

```math
i(a+bi) = -b + ai.
```

Voice B represents the same declared pair coordinates as a vector and applies

```math
R = \begin{pmatrix}0 & -1\\1 & 0\end{pmatrix},
```

so

```math
R\begin{pmatrix}a\\b\end{pmatrix}
= \begin{pmatrix}-b\\a\end{pmatrix}.
```

The frozen bridge identifies `a+bi` with `(a,b)` only for the declared comparison. Independent test-side oracles calculate the complex and matrix actions. Exact integer probes preserve the quarter-turn relation on the frozen specimen set.

This calibrates the receipt against a bridge whose mathematical relationship is known in advance. MathBand does not rediscover the isomorphism.

## Five Bats

### BAT-1 Rename

Symbols, voice names, probe names, order, and serialization-facing presentation are changed while mapped outputs are preserved.

Verified outcome: receipt semantics remain preserved. Mathematical equality does not depend on familiar names.

### BAT-2 Gauge

```text
common_integer_scale:7
```

Verified outcome: the declared quarter-turn relation remains exact while the representation transform survives separately in the receipt.

### BAT-3 Domain

Verified outcome:

```text
inside-domain  -> PRESERVED
outside-domain -> UNMAPPED
```

Undefined on the declared bridge is not silently treated as equality or falsity.

### BAT-4 Extra Voice

Voice B carries an additional declared structure:

```text
independent_conjugation_operation
```

Verified outcome: the exact shared probes remain preserved and the additional structure survives as `extra_b`. It is not projected away to improve apparent agreement.

A separate control removes one required assumption from the bridge declaration.

Verified outcome: the whole bridge is `refused` before probe grading with an explicit missing-assumption receipt.

### BAT-5 False Friend

The hostile sibling replaces the quarter-turn matrix with

```math
F = \begin{pmatrix}0 & 1\\1 & 0\end{pmatrix}.
```

For the frozen pair `(2,5)`, independent oracles predeclare five probes. Four are superficially compatible:

- input norm;
- output norm;
- fixed origin;
- four-step return.

The fifth probe is predeclared decisive:

```text
two-turn-output
```

Complex quarter-turn applied twice returns `(-2,-5)`. The reflection-like action applied twice returns `(2,5)`.

Verified outcome:

```text
4 PRESERVED
1 BROKEN
first_decisive_probe = two-turn-output
```

No aggregate match score exists to rescue the false bridge.

## Residual control

Numeric probes use an explicit nonnegative tolerance and retain absolute disagreement.

Frozen within-tolerance control:

```text
left      = 1.0
right     = 1.0005
tolerance = 0.001
```

Verified outcome: `RESIDUAL`, with the nonzero residual retained and receipt exactness marked `approximate`.

Frozen outside-tolerance control:

```text
left      = 1.0
right     = 1.01
tolerance = 0.001
```

Verified outcome: `BROKEN`; because the probe is predeclared decisive, it becomes the first decisive failure.

Therefore:

```text
WITHIN TOLERANCE != EXACT.
BEST OR CLOSE ENOUGH != ZERO RESIDUAL.
```

## Documented mathematics

For real integers `a,b`, multiplication of `a+bi` by `i` corresponds under the declared coordinate identification to the linear map `(a,b) -> (-b,a)` represented by `[[0,-1],[1,0]]`.

That quarter-turn preserves `a^2+b^2`, composes to negation after two turns, and returns to identity after four turns.

The hostile matrix `[[0,1],[1,0]]` also preserves Euclidean norm and fixes the origin, and its fourth power is identity, but its square is identity rather than negation. Those shared coarse properties are therefore insufficient to establish the declared quarter-turn bridge.

## Dogram inference

A bridge receipt is more useful when it preserves the shape of disagreement instead of collapsing comparison into one similarity score.

For this finite incubator:

- exact structure can survive renaming and a declared common representation transform;
- bridge validity can be local to a domain;
- unmatched structure can coexist with preserved shared probes;
- missing assumptions can make a bridge unevaluable without making either theory false;
- a predeclared decisive probe can reject a superficially strong false friend;
- nonzero numeric residue can survive a tolerated comparison rather than being rounded into exact equality.

These are properties of the declared finite comparison contract. They do not establish that arbitrary mathematical disciplines can be automatically bridged.
