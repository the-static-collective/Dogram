# DECLARED-PATH-EQUIVALENCE-001

Status: bounded research specimen. Authority: none. Public operator: none.

## Question

After PARALLEL-ARROW-FACTORIZATION-001, what survives when two distinct parallel paths are deliberately declared equivalent?

## Exact specimen

Start with the free one-object path category on generators `a,b`, bounded to words of length <= 4 for exhaustive replay. The paths

`p = aa` and `q = bb`

are literally distinct words. Declare one relation/witness `alpha: aa ~ bb`, then compute the least bounded two-sided congruence generated by it. The quotient puts `aa` and `bb` in the same class, and compatibility propagates the relation through contexts, e.g. `aaa ~ abb` and `baa ~ bbb`.

A hostile control declaring only the reflexive generator `aa ~ aa` leaves `aa` and `bb` distinct.

## Delta

Before declaration: `aa != bb`.

After quotient by the declared congruence: `[aa] = [bb]`.

The relation receipt `alpha` remains separate data explaining why the quotient may identify the two paths.

**DECLARED PATH EQUIVALENCE != PATH IDENTITY.**

Secondary seal: **A RELATION MAY AUTHORIZE TWO PATHS TO COMPUTE AS ONE. IT DOES NOT MAKE THEIR SUPPLIED RECEIPTS HAVE ALWAYS BEEN ONE.**

## Documented mathematics

Poinsot & Caenepeel (2013), DOI `10.1155/2013/370618`, explicitly constructs free categories from directed graphs with arrows given by composable paths. This supplies the unreduced path side.

Wolfram `AbstractCategory` documentation independently distinguishes a free category generated by a quiver from the reduced category obtained after explicitly imposing morphism equivalences; its examples retain parallel morphisms before equivalence and expose reduced morphisms afterward.

Higher-category literature motivates treating relations between morphisms as additional morphism-level structure rather than literal identity; Jurco, Saemann, Schreiber & Wolf (2019), DOI `10.1002/prop.201910001`, describes 2-morphisms as morphisms between morphisms.

## Dogram inference

If a downstream calculation consumes quotient equality, the receipt should be able to distinguish at least:

1. the supplied paths;
2. the declared relation/rewrite that licenses identification;
3. the resulting quotient class.

Collapsing all three into one equality bit loses the provenance of the identification.

## Refusals

- REWRITE/2-CELL != OCCURRENCE.
- QUOTIENT EQUALITY != EVIDENTIARY EQUALITY.
- CONGRUENCE CLOSURE != CAUSAL CLOSURE.
- DECLARED RELATION != SEMANTIC TRUTH.
- ALGEBRAIC PERMISSION != AUTHORITY OUTSIDE THE DECLARED CALCULATION.
