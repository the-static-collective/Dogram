## 1. Three strands do not automatically imply three states

```text

```

```text

```

All bijective assignments `S -> P` form `S3`, so there are:





Disposition:

> **THREE STRANDS + THREE POSITIONS != THREE-STATE ORBIT.**

> `Phi_3` requires the additional cyclic-order constraint.
